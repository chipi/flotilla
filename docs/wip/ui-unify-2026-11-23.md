# One UI: every page reviewed for weight, action and consistency (2026-11-23)

The operator, after Now and Autonomy were reworked: the top of every page holds what they act on
or most need; secondary status is folded or paged, never unbounded; every table that can grow
sorts, filters and pages like the rest; consistency above everything. Now and Autonomy are the
reference, with Hosts, Runs, Cases and History for tables.

A read-only audit screenshotted the 40 snapshot pages at 1440 and 390 wide and read the
templates of pages without a snapshot. Its findings, checked against the code before each change:

## The rules every page follows

1. **The first screen is for acting.** A decision, a list to work through, or the one fact that is
   wrong leads. Counters (score cards) are one compact row, never a block before the work.
2. **Nothing grows without bound on screen.** A list that grows is a table with the standard
   (`table.go`: sortable `Th` headers, pills, search, pager), or shows a few with the rest folded.
3. **Filters sit under the title,** pills then search, as on Hosts, Cases and History.
4. **Red only for state,** paired with its word; no red at rest, no red for a trend.
5. **A sortable-looking header sorts.** No arrow on a column that does not.

## Left as they are, and why

Bounded by configuration or by a cap in the code, so short by nature: Models' three tables
(aliases, fleets), a run's model calls (capped at 12 with "Show all"), a run's episodes, a
routine's last runs (12), a host's allowlist (the registry), Knowledge's sources, Examples, Pair,
Users (people an admin adds).

## Built (2026-11-23)

All eleven rows above, plus three the review turned up:

- **The fleet board** draws "host agents" only on a fleet with an action that a host agent runs on
  one of its case kinds (`fleetUsesHostAgents`), so one host that is down is no longer drawn on
  every fleet.
- **Approvals** opens the first waiting plan; the rest are one line each (id, risk, title, how long
  it waits), folded, each opening to its full decision. The separate "Jump to a plan" list is gone.
- **Snapshots** now render Knowledge, the brief, its archive and Examples, with notes and a brief
  seeded.

## Not covered

- Approvals keeps the order the case list gives; it does not sort by risk or age.
- The brief's sections (Handled overnight, up to 10 rows) are fixed lists, not tables.

## The ledger explorer (2026-11-23)

The operator: the platform keeps a ledger, so there must be a way to explore what is inside it, as
the place of last resort. History showed case events and the triage ledger but nothing of the
`events` table (on the installation's store, 1,703 rows of 27 kinds: autonomy changes, pauses,
hooks, deploys, sign-ins, `person.*`). Record → Ledger (`/ledger`, `store.Explore`) reads the three
streams as one table: platform (`events`), cases (`case_events`, the status change leading the
detail), fleet ledgers (`ledger_entries`, the row as written). Filters: stream (with counts), period
or a from/to date, kind (each with its count under the other filters), actor, text over every field
and the raw record. `ref` follows one thing through every stream: a row whose subject, case, run,
actor or occurrence is exactly it. Each row opens to its full record; the view exports as JSON (up
to 10,000 rows). Read by the roles that read the ledger in History.

Entry points, "See in the ledger": a case, a run, a routine (`triage/cycle`), a fleet, a host
(`host:<name>`), an action on Autonomy (`action:<name>`), every autonomy change, a user
(`user:<name>`), and History's header.

Not covered: no page links a hook (`hook:<name>`) or a person by email yet, since no page shows
either; a run's model calls and spans stay on the run's page.
