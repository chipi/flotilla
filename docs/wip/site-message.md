# The site's message (draft 2)

Built on [positioning.md](positioning.md). The site says "agents"; the product says "routine".

## Hero

**Put AI agents to work on your business processes, and stay in control.**

flotilla is where a business runs its agents and governs them: every decision recorded, every bit of
autonomy earned, a person wherever it matters.

## The problem

Agents are easy to demo and hard to trust. A model is not deterministic; a business process has to be
dependable. Most tools help you build an agent. Few help you decide when to let it act.

## What we believe

An agent is a business process with a model inside it. You already know how to trust a process: you
see what it does, it works within rules, and its remit widens only as it earns it. Agents deserve the
same discipline, not hype.

## How trust is built

Inside each agent: its instructions, its tools, its limits, checks on every tool it calls.

Around it, flotilla adds what one agent cannot give itself:

1. **Code decides, not the model.** The agent proposes; rules you can read decide what leaves.
2. **Autonomy is earned, one action at a time.** Every action starts by recording what it would do,
   then asks a person, and acts alone only on its record.
3. **A person approves what cannot be undone.** A sent email, a merge, a launch: always a person.
4. **You see everything.** Every decision, model call and approval, with who made it and why.
5. **Changes are proven first.** A new rule is replayed against your own past cases before it ships.
6. **You can always stop it.** One process, one fleet, or everything.

## Where we stand

- **Code coordinates agents.** Agents hand work to each other through recorded cases, not through a
  model directing other models: every handoff can be audited.
- **Autonomy is earned, never configured.**
- **Measure on your own work, not on benchmarks.** Shadow runs on real cases, replays of real history,
  checks of every model you use. (Link: eval-harness, how to do evals.)

## What it runs

Fleets of agents for sales ops, marketing, outbound, customer success, operations and engineering: the
example fleets, each a starting point for your own.

## Start

`make demo`: the platform and its example fleets on your machine, nothing reaching the internet.
