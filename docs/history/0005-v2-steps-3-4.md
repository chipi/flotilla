**Outcome:** A routine version can declare stations, checked against the harnesses' declared
capabilities and handed to a run as `FLOTILLA_STATIONS`. The harness layer is the platform's, in
`runtime/`: one adapter interface without roles, pi serving every `tools` value, the seams and a
seam end-to-end that runs on the real pi and, in CI, on a fake harness. Every adapter's episode is
validated against `runtime/episode.schema.json`. Built on `main`, not pushed. Design:
[v2 design](../wip/v2-design.md) sections 2, 3 and 6, decided in
[ADR-0020](../adr/ADR-0020-routine-versions-stations-harnesses.md).

## Decisions worth knowing

- **The shared module is `runtime/adapter.py`,** because `runtime/harness.py` is triage's in-process
  loop and keeps its name.
- **The bugfix chain is not switched onto the generic pi adapter.** Its leaves, its review call
  (`bugfix/pi_review.py`) and `golden_pi_commands.json` are unchanged; its episode files stay
  version 1 with `role`. The switch is the installation's v2 work, replayed.
- **`flotilla` is declared, not an adapter:** `adapter.IN_PROCESS` gives the in-process loop its row
  in `fleets/harnesses.json` (serves `all` and `none`, has hooks).
- **Nothing live changes until a routine declares stations.** No shipped routine does; the C0
  goldens stayed green at every commit; `stations` is not a `legacyOwned` key.
- **eval-harness's generic episode schema does not exist yet.** The superset relation is pending:
  no pinned copy and no placeholder, the validator takes a list of schemas, and adding theirs is one
  entry (and a checker extension if it needs `$ref`).
- **The offline run is proxy and credentials, not a kernel boundary:** the deploy's service
  network is what stops a process that ignores the proxy variables.

## Found on the way

- **A process a tool detaches outlived the run.** The seam end-to-end found that pi's bash tool puts
  `nohup sleep 300 &` in a session of its own, out of the group the runner kills. `run_process` now
  tags every process it starts (`FLOTILLA_RUN_TAG`) and, after the group kill, kills what carries the
  tag or descends from the run. Its one gap is a process that clears its own environment; a child
  subreaper would catch that and is not used. Linux only: without `/proc` only the group is killed.
- **The behaviour change reaches every caller of `run_process`** (the bugfix chain's `fix.sh`
  included), on kill paths only: a timeout, a turn cap, the caller's exit.
- **`/tmp` filled up.** The test runs left thousands of temp folders on a shared tmpfs of about a
  million inodes. `make test-unit` now runs under `scripts/with-tmp.sh`. On a host where mise shims
  run, a base below `$HOME` makes the seam end-to-end's proxy see `api.github.com`; such a host sets
  `FLOTILLA_TEST_TMP_BASE` outside `$HOME`.

## Not a strict no-op

- **The bugfix worker image gains `runtime/`** (`COPY runtime`), an image change.
- **Schema 32** adds `run_calls.station`, `run_calls.texts` and the `call_texts` table. It is
  additive and old rows read as before, but a store at 31 is migrated at the next start.
- **`run_process` sweeps detached processes on kill paths** and tags every child's environment.
