Autonomy

What each routine may do on its own: shadow (nothing outward), propose (asks first), auto. A routine never goes above its spec's cap, and while the freeze holds every routine runs at shadow whatever is set. Change it on the fleet's page; raising it needs a reason. STOP stops a fleet's runs entirely.
